The loop

One loop, six steps, every one of them on the phone

Discover

Reward pins on a live map. Alerts when a mission lands within your radius.

Reserve

A slot is held for 45 minutes on chain. Gasless for the worker.

Walk

Live distance, navigate intent, and a green state the moment you are inside the radius.

Prove

In-app camera only. The photo carries GPS, accuracy, timestamp and device signals.

Verify

Location policy, freshness, duplicate detection, then a vision judge with named checks.

Get paid

approve_completion moves USDC from the vault to your wallet and writes the proof hash.

Score, streak and earnings update from chain, and the map already shows the next one.

Why Solana

The chain is the database

Missions, escrow, reservations, completions, reputation, streaks and stake are accounts of one Anchor program. The verifier holds no state.

Mission + vault

A PDA per mission with its own USDC token account. The creator funds reward × slots in one signature.

Escrow you can inspect

"$12.00 still locked" on the mission page is the vault balance.

Reservation

Held for a worker by the verifier's signature. The worker never pays a fee.

Instant global settlement

A worker in Paris is paid by a creator anywhere, in seconds, with a transaction link.

Completion

Proof hash, confidence and the yes/no answer, written in the same transaction as the payout.

Verifier is a key, not a database

It can only pay a reserved worker from that mission's vault, once.

Why Seeker

Seeker hardware gets real marketplace utility

Seeker verified badge

The app inspects the wallet's Token-2022 accounts for a Seeker Genesis Token (mint authority, metadata pointer, token group), then the verifier binds it on chain.

One device, one worker

A Device PDA maps each Genesis Token mint to a single wallet. Sponsored campaigns pay once per Seeker, not once per keypair.

Seeker-only missions

Creators tick "Seeker only"; the program refuses approvals from unbound wallets.

Verified tier from day one

A bound Seeker skips the Explorer tier: priority verification and a badge on the leaderboard.

Trust and privacy

Every proof passes named checks the worker can see

  • ✓At the mission locationdistance ≤ radius + min(accuracy, 50 m) + 25 m
  • ✓GPS accuracy≤ 100 m, mock-location flag rejected
  • ✓Fresh capturetimestamp within 10 min of server time
  • ✓Original photoSHA-256 not on chain, dhash Hamming > 6 from recent
  • ✓Live photo, not a screenvision judge flags re-photographed screens and prints
  • ✓Photo matches the missionrelevant, fulfils the instructions, confidence ≥ 60
  • ✓2 to 4 mission-specific checkse.g. "accepted payment methods are readable"

Privacy by construction

Photos are judged in memory and discarded. Only the hash goes on chain. Precise location is read only while a mission screen is open.

Keys stay in the wallet

Sign-in, funding and staking go through Mobile Wallet Adapter. The verifier key can only pay a reserved worker from that mission's vault.
